# PSEPageType

The kind of content a reader screen shows: a page, article, or advert
from either edition system, or other content such as an image gallery.

```
@objc enum PSEPageType
```

## Topics

### Feed Edition Pages

[`PSEPageType.Page`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/Page)

A page of an edition on PageSuite’s current system.

[`PSEPageType.Article`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/Article)

An article of an edition on PageSuite’s current system.

[`PSEPageType.PDF`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/PDF)

A PDF page.

[`PSEPageType.Advert`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/Advert)

An advert page.

### Other Content

[`PSEPageType.BookmarkArticle`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/BookmarkArticle)

A saved article opened from bookmarks.

[`PSEPageType.Other`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/Other)

Content that isn’t an edition page or article, such as the page
browser, an image gallery, or a web page.

### Replica Editions

[`PSEPageType.ReplicaPage`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/ReplicaPage)

A page of a legacy replica edition.

[`PSEPageType.ReplicaArticle`](/documentation/PSReader/PSEPageType/ReplicaArticle)

An article of a legacy replica edition.
