# PSReader

Embed digital newspaper and magazine editions in your iOS app. Use PageSuite’s prebuilt reader, or build your own interface on the same engine.

## Overview

PSReader shows editions from PageSuite’s current edition system: classic
PDF editions, laid out like print, and responsive editions alike.
Integrate it one of two ways. The **prebuilt UI** is a complete reader
app you configure and brand. A **custom UI** is your own interface on the
core SDK, built around the same page renderer. Both work with every
edition on that system, classic PDF included, and one app can mix them.
The prebuilt UI also shows editions from PageSuite’s legacy replica
system; [Edition Platforms](/documentation/PSReader/Edition-Platforms) explains the two systems.

[Prebuilt UI](/documentation/PSReader/Prebuilt-UI)

A complete reader app you configure: edition archives, the reader, downloads, bookmarks, and search, styled to match your brand.

[Custom UI](/documentation/PSReader/Custom-UI)

Build your own reader screens on the SDK’s core: your archive, your reader screen, and the features you choose to add.
